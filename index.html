The journey began before sunrise — bags packed, excitement at an all-time high. We boarded our flight from Zamboanga City and touched down at Ninoy Aquino International Airport in the early hours of the morning, officially kicking off our week-long IT academic adventure.

Old Manila City Tour 🏛️

After settling in, we dove straight into Manila's rich heritage. Our afternoon tour covered three iconic landmarks:

  • Rizal Park — We stood at the monument of José Rizal, taking in the sprawling Luneta grounds.
  • Intramuros — The ancient walled city. Cobblestone streets, centuries-old walls, and history at every corner.
  • Manila Cathedral — A breathtaking Baroque-Romanesque basilica at the heart of Intramuros since 1571.

Freedom Day at Tagaytay 🌋

With a national holiday giving us a well-deserved break, we escaped Metro Manila for the cool highlands of Tagaytay. The itinerary? Pure fun.

  • People's Park in the Sky — Perched at over 700 meters, we soaked in panoramic views of Taal Lake and Volcano. The mist, the breeze, the views — absolutely magical.
  • Sky Ranch Tagaytay — Thrills, rides, and laughter. The Sky Eye gave us a bird's eye view of the entire Tagaytay ridge.

See You Again, Manila 👋

All good journeys must come to an end. Our final day brought us back to the airport for our flight home to Zamboanga City. Bags heavier with pasalubong, hearts even heavier with gratitude — this tour gave us more than academic exposure. It gave us perspective, inspiration, and friendships forged through shared experiences.

From Intramuros' ancient walls to Tagaytay's breezy heights, from animation studios to traffic command centers — every moment was a lesson. Every visit a revelation.

"We leave not as students who visited companies, but as future IT professionals who glimpsed their destiny."

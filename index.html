Day 01
April 5

Zamboanga → Manila

First steps into the capital

🌅 Morning
Ninoy Aquino International Airport, Manila

Bound for Manila ✈️

The journey began before sunrise — bags packed, excitement at an all-time high. We boarded our flight from Zamboanga City and touched down at Ninoy Aquino International Airport in the early hours of the morning, officially kicking off our week-long IT academic adventure.

✈️ Travel🌆 Manila
☀️ Afternoon
Rizal Park — the heart of historic Manila

Old Manila City Tour 🏛️

After settling in, we dove straight into Manila's rich heritage. Our afternoon tour covered three iconic landmarks:

  • Rizal Park — We stood at the monument of the Philippines' national hero, José Rizal, taking in the sprawling Luneta grounds.
  • Intramuros — The ancient walled city that once housed the seat of Spanish colonial power. Cobblestone streets, centuries-old walls, and history at every corner.
  • Manila Cathedral — A breathtaking Baroque-Romanesque basilica standing at the heart of Intramuros since 1571.
🏛️ Heritage📸 Sightseeing🇵🇭 History

Holiday at Tagaytay 🎉

Rest, recharge, and take in the views

🎊 Holiday
People's Park in the Sky, Tagaytay
Sky Ranch Tagaytay — rides above the clouds

Freedom Day at Tagaytay 🌋

With a national holiday giving us a well-deserved break, we escaped Metro Manila for the cool highlands of Tagaytay.

  • People's Park in the Sky — Perched atop a hill at over 700 meters, we soaked in panoramic views of Taal Lake and Volcano.
  • Sky Ranch Tagaytay — Thrills, rides, and laughter. The Sky Eye gave us a bird's eye view of the entire Tagaytay ridge.
🎡 Leisure🌿 Tagaytay🌋 Taal View🎊 Holiday
